Columns and properties are labelled with the property's name, as in Obsidian (`priority`, `file name`, `my_property`), instead of a title-cased version (`Priority`, `Name`, `My Property`). The new `propertyLabels` option brings the title-cased labels back (`propertyLabels: title`). A property's `displayName` is used either way.
